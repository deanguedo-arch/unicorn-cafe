The new user request is a restaurant cooking spinoff. FUTURE-GAME-STARTER.md
explicitly permits changing mechanics while preserving the universe. This
game therefore uses direct cooking rather than walking, collecting, blasting,
jailing or stealth. No existing adventure build or project setting was edited.

Actual source pixels were viewed before art decisions: original unicorn hero,
main unicorn pose atlas, v14 babies, homeowner/worker sheets, restaurant map,
and extracted embedded production artwork from the latest v14 source HTML.

Reused actual source art:
- The large title unicorn is a full happy/walking pose from
  core-original/unicorn-sprites-cutout.png, isolated without repainting.
- The chef's small idle/happy sprites use the current embedded DATA.u_idle0
  and DATA.u_happy0 artwork, not a newly invented replacement.
- Pink/blue baby idle/happy/celebration states use the current v14 baby sheet.
  Text labels were excluded by reviewed crops. The blue celebration crop was
  corrected after the first visual review to preserve its full horn.
- The smiling human customer uses current embedded DATA.h_happy artwork.
- easy_restaurant.png supplies the cozy restaurant backdrop. It remains
  behind the customer region and subtle title texture, away from the worktop.
- Original unicorn-hero.png supplies the app icons and identity reference.

There is no file or embedded key literally named mainunicornsprites in this
ZIP. Its current equivalent source keys and original pose sheet were resolved
and used. This was not an art-access blocker.

The embedded source also has small food icons, which were extracted and
viewed. Large close-up cooking needs matching preparation states, utensils
and ingredients. Four new original transparent food atlases were generated
with the built-in image tool, using the viewed canon as reference. These keep
one coherent food treatment across menu, worktop and finished dish. Their
42 isolated production sprites include all seven meals and their needed
intermediate states. Full exact prompts and original atlas PNGs are retained.

No third-party characters, artwork, music, maps or branded identity were used.
Interface symbols are original simple SVG paths. Kitchenware geometry,
liquids, frosting motion, sparkles and steam are drawn by the Canvas renderer.
The actual food and characters use supplied/generated bitmap artwork.

Asset optimization only crops/isolate sprite frames, removes unrelated alpha
specks outside the selected frames, adds padding, and downsizes when needed.
Production food sprites use lossless WebP at up to 320px. The large title pose
is 447x500 without upscaling; room art is 1280x782. The provenance manifests
record paths, source keys, crop rectangles, dimensions and SHA256 hashes.

Character motion is playful and non-blocking. Cooking has no countdown or
precision requirement. All recipes are available; a seven-dish order bag
guarantees the first cycle covers the full menu, then reshuffles each cycle.
Wrong food and restart preserve earned stickers. All saving is local-only.

Validation evidence belongs to the supplied test scripts/results and
TEST-REPORT.txt. Native Canvas rendering is not represented as an iPhone or
browser screenshot, and simulated DOM checks are not represented as Safari
or touchscreen-device tests.
