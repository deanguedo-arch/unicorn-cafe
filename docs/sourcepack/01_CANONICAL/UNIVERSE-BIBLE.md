# Sneaky Unicorn — Universe Bible

## Origin
Sneaky Unicorn began as a game idea from a five-year-old child. The emotional target is more important than genre purity: **funny, magical, easy to understand, kind, collectible, and replayable**.

## Core fantasy
You are a small rainbow unicorn entering playful environments, stealing/collecting funny treasures, dodging or blasting obstacles, discovering magical secret spaces, rescuing baby unicorns, and bringing things home.

A separate **Police Unicorn** fantasy turns the unicorn into the hero who rounds up silly bad guys with rainbow magic.

## Tone
- Cute, mischievous, energetic.
- Never frightening or punitive.
- Halloween content is whimsical, toy-like, squashy, and silly rather than horror.
- Failure should look like sparkles, plops, boops, wobbling toys, rainbow bubbles, or playful resets.

## Audience
Primary playtest audience: young children, roughly age 5–9.

Design for:
- imperfect finger control,
- little or no required reading,
- obvious goals,
- large touch targets,
- fast reward feedback,
- short recoveries after mistakes,
- high visual delight.

## Current cast
### Mom / Main Unicorn
White unicorn with rainbow mane/tail and golden horn. Bright, readable, friendly silhouette.

### Baby Unicorns
Small pastel variants. Each rescue should feel emotionally rewarding. They follow Mom Unicorn in a line like ducklings behind a parent.

### Normal-map people
Friendly homeowner/store/restaurant/toy-store people. They follow patrol patterns. They are obstacles, not villains.

### Halloween Dumpling Bad Guys
Squishy toy-like creatures inspired by cute seasonal dumpling toys, not realistic monsters. Current themes include pumpkin, eyeball, ghost, candy corn, purple monster, and a large king/boss dumpling.

## Design pillars
1. **Immediate control** — movement should feel obvious within seconds.
2. **Collecting is fun** — lots of visible, funny things to grab.
3. **Magic is physical** — doors appear, portals flare, babies follow, dumplings plop.
4. **Mistakes are funny** — no harsh punishment loops.
5. **One-screen readability** — every important object should read clearly on a phone.
6. **Replay rewards** — secrets, babies, costumes, treasure collection, alternate paths.
