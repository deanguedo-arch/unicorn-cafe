# Reference / IP Guide

The project has used games such as **Zombies Ate My Neighbors**, Mario, and Sonic as shorthand for design qualities: immediacy, readable action, colorful maps, responsiveness, and playful pacing.

For future games:
- Use those references only for high-level design principles.
- Keep Sneaky Unicorn's characters, maps, UI, art, names, sound, level layouts, and mechanics expression original.
- Do not reproduce proprietary levels, sprites, music, logos, character likenesses, or story material.
- Halloween dumpling characters should be original cute squishy characters inspired by the general category of soft seasonal toys, not copies of a specific branded product.
