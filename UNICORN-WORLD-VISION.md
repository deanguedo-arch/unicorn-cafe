# Unicorn World vision

Unicorn World is a walkable shared neighbourhood connecting the existing games and a mall. The restaurant leads into the unicorn café; home leads into Sneaky Unicorn. The reserved upper path leads to a shopping street and mall concourse, with separate boutique, fitting room, wheels shop, arcade and photo booth scenes. A future cleaner job can visit the existing houses, restaurant and grocery store, plus the mall.

Keep a coherent world through approved consistent artwork, palette, outlines, perspective and scale, canonical character/asset reuse, the same unicorn drag movement feel and a bottom-right contextual action button. Mechanics vary with each activity.

The local candidate in hub/ implements the first shared-world loop: play the café or adventure, earn coins, buy clothes or equipment, and use the same outfit across actual play. A travelling backpack holds permanent purchases and three outfit cards. Bikes and skateboards work on the outdoor routes and free practice track, and park indoors. The arcade offers Star Catcher, Bowling and 48 distinct illustrated jigsaws at 4, 9 or 16 pieces; finished rounds earn cosmetic-prize stamps. Native progress and independent launches remain intact.

Phone play remains landscape with a persistent bottom-right main action. Portrait suspends and clears controls, then restores the activity. Saving is local to the usual browser/device; automatic sync and standalone-to-world earnings reconciliation are future work. The candidate has not been published. Her actual-device review, shopping clarity and picture-only puzzle resumption still need human review.
