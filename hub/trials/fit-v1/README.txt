UNICORN WORLD SMALL CLOTHING FIT TRIAL

Start with previews/FIVE_POSE_FIT.jpg and previews/MOVEMENT_REGISTRATION.gif. The original sprites and separate transparent layers are included for review and later integration.

For a quick look, open previews/FIVE_POSE_FIT.jpg and previews/MOVEMENT_REGISTRATION.gif. ORIGINAL_AND_TRIAL.jpg compares the untouched base stages with the dressed trial.

CONTENTS
15 independent transparent wearable layer PNGs, plus one exact-original bike foreground occlusion layer: tee, beads and bow for u_idle0, u_run2, u_happy1, bike0 and skateboard0. Ten right/left composite PNGs. Original base stages and source art, generated layer sources, exact registration metadata, generation prompts and pixel-check results.

WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES
The shirt has pose-specific neckline and foreleg cutouts. The necklace is a shallow collar below the muzzle. The original bow remains separate. Final characters are composed from the original base sprites; generated dressed references were used only to derive the clothing. No character redraw is used as the final base.

REGISTRATION AND OCCLUSION
Use the full 512x512 canvas for every layer. Read per-frame pivots and order from manifest.json. Translate, scale, rotate and mirror the whole actor group. Never resize a cropped shirt to a generic body rectangle. The visible-cloth cutouts already preserve mane and foreleg occlusion; the old oval sleeve patches and generic face polygon should not be added on top. For bike0, draw the original-pixel grip/forearm foreground after tee and before beads; its exact mask contract is included. Keep existing source art, item IDs, prices, ownership and saves.

LIMITS
This is a five-pose visual fit trial, not a production wardrobe release. Only one running stride, one celebration pose and one frame of each vehicle are included. The GIF demonstrates group movement and mirroring, not complete walking or pedaling animation. Fourteen other pose states for this outfit remain unproduced. The new fabric is crisper than the low-resolution original ground sprites. Live-game integration and physical-device testing have not been performed.

NEXT DECISION
Review this small trial before approving the remaining animation frames or other clothing items. Any live-game implementation is a separate step.
