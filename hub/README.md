# Unicorn World and Mall

The main GitHub Pages entry is [Unicorn World](https://deanguedo-arch.github.io/unicorn-cafe/). The same repository retains independent café and adventure launches beneath `game/build/` and `adventure/`. Dean explicitly authorized this replacement release after the local visual review.

Run `python3 hub_preview.py` from the repository root, or double-click `Play-World.command`. Open [Unicorn World](http://localhost:8790/hub/?legacy=1). Stop the preview with Control-C. Nothing is published by this launcher.

Drag anywhere to move, using the café's invisible joystick displacement /55 and 292 world-units/second. Arrow keys or WASD also work. Tap a doorway or its hoofprints to walk toward it, then use the persistent bottom-right action picture, Space or Enter. The entrance area is generous and shows a soft highlight nearby; walking near a door alone never enters. Each place has a small illustrated exit arrow: tap it to walk there, or walk onto it and stop briefly to return. The action button can also return immediately. Arriving or reloading on an exit does not send her straight back. Phone play stays in landscape; portrait pauses, clears held controls and restores the same activity when turned back. Main shop, puzzle-start, photo and bowling actions also stay at the lower right.

The restaurant opens the actual café; home opens the actual v15 adventure. Follow the upper neighbourhood path to the shopping street and mall. Its concourse leads into separate boutique, fitting room, wheels shop, arcade and photo booth scenes. The shops use picture categories, free try-on, permanent ownership and a travelling backpack with clothes, three saved outfit cards and equipment. One picture wishlist records the current saving goal; the free photo booth holds 24 local outfit photos.

## Coins, wardrobe and arcade

The first connected café launch imports its existing coin total once. Its native coin counter continues recording earned history; world purchases spend only the shared wallet. Each café payment earns one world coin and each legitimate adventure completion, including replay, earns three. Independent launches retain native saves and costumes; standalone earnings reconciliation is later work.

Three clothing pieces are free and nine are purchasable: headwear/accessories cost 3 and bodywear costs 6. Trying unaffordable clothes is free. Leaving the fitting room or boutique restores the owned outfit unless a piece was bought or an owned combination explicitly worn. Earned adventure costume pieces respect their original secret thresholds and become permanently available in the backpack. Shared clothes use eleven pose anchors and the correct cropped-sprite or padded-atlas scale during walking, carried-object play and celebrations in both games. Necklaces and scarves sit below the muzzle, ordinary garments/accessories are clipped behind the original face/mane, and helmets leave the eyes clear. Riding poses use the same fitting rules. The world sound button also mutes both connected games without changing their native saved sound preferences.

The skateboard costs 15 coins and gives a 20% boost; the bike costs 25 and gives 35%. Choose either for a free practice ride before buying. Owned vehicles work on the neighbourhood routes, shopping street and test track, park automatically in other interiors, and stay owned. They do not change adventure movement or treasure capacity.

Every paid arcade round costs one earned coin. Instruction pictures and playable Star Catcher/Bowling demonstrations are free; demonstrations grant no coins or stamps. Star Catcher has 20 stars over 30 seconds: 8 catches return one coin and 15 return two. Bowling has three throws, with ten pins reset each throw: 10 total pins return one coin and 20 return two. The jigsaw wall has 48 distinct illustrated compositions, eight each for unicorns, animals, food, outfits, vehicles and locations. Each supports 4, 9 and 16 pieces, drag/snapping, optional guide and no timer. Piece-grid pictures accompany the difficulty choices. Each picture/difficulty saves separately; an unfinished puzzle resumes free, completion returns its entry coin, and replay starts a new paid round. Catcher and bowling also resume interrupted paid sessions.

Every completed round earns one stamp regardless of score. Choose one of six exclusive cosmetics; five stamps grant that prize permanently. Finishing, earning, buying and repeated acknowledgements cannot pay or charge twice.

## Save origins and recovery

The usual local review URL keeps the existing café origin `http://localhost:8787` and adventure origin `http://127.0.0.1:8788`. Keep these hostnames exactly: browsers separate their saves. The launcher starts those servers if they are not running. Their native keys stay `sneaky-unicorn-restaurant-v1-4` and `sneaky-unicorn-midnight-v15`; original payload schemas and older save namespaces remain intact. Native progress stays at each native origin.

The hub origin owns one IndexedDB database, `unicorn-world-profile-v1`, with profile and transaction-receipt stores. Money and ownership commit together; purchasing and wearing can commit together too. The existing hub navigation key remains `unicorn-world-hub-v1`. Each connected game adds a separate `unicorn-world-cafe-bridge-v1` or `unicorn-world-adventure-bridge-v1` localStorage sidecar for stable event IDs, pending rewards and a native-write journal. Acknowledged parent/iframe messages check the actual window and origin. Sidecars retry until the hub commits and acknowledges. Resetting a native game rotates its earning generation without deleting purchases, outbox receipts or the one-time import record.

Return/reentry within one tab keeps the live iframe, unfinished cooking and adventure position. The hub waits for the game's pause/save acknowledgement. The café and all nine adventure maps have reachable connected-world exit arrows, separate from their native completion goals. The café exit is beyond the dining-table interaction areas; all five table actions remain usable. Pausing, rotation and reentry clear exit dwell so a resumed game does not immediately leave. Opening the backpack also suspends the native game and resumes its prior mode. Reload retains native saved progress and paid arcade sessions; the original adventure does not save a partly played level across reload, and that rule is unchanged. Failed world storage prevents purchases and retains recoverable pending earnings rather than showing a false success.

On GitHub Pages, both frames and their shared scripts/assets use the checked `/unicorn-cafe/` project base while messages still check the parent window and origin. Existing café saves at the former root are available to the connected café because localStorage belongs to the same browser origin. The replacement root worker retires only that old café root cache, leaving native saves, the world IndexedDB profile and sibling caches intact. The café reloads for updates to its own worker, without restarting when it first acquires its separate worker scope.
