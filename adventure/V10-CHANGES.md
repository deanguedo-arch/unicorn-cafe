# Sneaky Unicorn v10 — Changes

- Every treasure in the four collection maps is relocated onto reachable walkable floor; no raised/JUMP-only loot remains.
- Each collection map now has two patrol people.
- Patrol speeds vary at runtime using per-person min/max ranges and independent change timers.
- Added a translucent in-game Home/Menu button with a large touch target.
- Contact no longer instantly teleports the unicorn. It starts a ~1.08 s sparkly rainbow send-back animation; treasure is preserved and patrols continue.
- Police Unicorn bad guys now require three blast hits each.
- Police bad guys have distinct runtime robber costumes (beanies, eye masks, striped vests, different colors) and three visible hit stars.
- First and second police hits briefly stun with sparkles; third hit sends the bad guy permanently to jail.
- Normal adventure blast still temporarily sends people to the jail lane before they resume their route.
- Cache/save/version identifiers bumped to v10.
