# V15 Canon Rules

- Parent carry capacity: 1 item.
- Each rescued baby adds exactly 1 carrying slot.
- With both babies following, family capacity is 3 items.
- Regular adventure opponents stay jailed until ALL regular opponents are jailed, then release together to patrol.
- Police Unicorn captures stay jailed permanently.
- Rainbow Blast uses the last meaningful movement direction and preserves it while idle.
- No arbitrary side-shot fallback.
