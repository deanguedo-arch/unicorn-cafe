# Sneaky Unicorn v14 — Magic Doors & Halloween Squishies

## Integrated generated art
- New high-end magical door animation art is used for both secret doors in every treasure level.
- First-level door exit logic was repaired so returning from a secret room places the unicorn away from the trigger instead of immediately re-entering.
- Two separate secret rooms per treasure level use the generated enchanted room background.
- Two baby unicorns per treasure level use generated pink and blue motion sprites.
- Rescued babies follow behind the main unicorn in a delayed duckling-style line.
- Rainbow portal/slide pads now use generated portal effect artwork and stronger capture/travel flare.
- Police Unicorn now uses the generated Halloween festival map.
- Five police opponents are generated-style Halloween squishy dumplings: pumpkin, eyeball, ghost, candy corn, and purple monster. Each requires three rainbow-blast hits.
- King Dumpling appears after the five regular dumplings are jailed and requires five hits.
- Jailed dumplings use the generated cage states.
