# Sneaky Unicorn v12 — Secret Sparkle Rescue

## New kid-focused features
- Hidden sparkle room on each of the four regular adventure maps.
- One baby unicorn to rescue in each hidden room; rescued babies persist in local save data.
- After the first rescue, a tiny baby unicorn follows the player cosmetically.
- One rainbow trap-door / slide on every regular map. Stepping on it launches the unicorn in a sparkly rainbow ride to another open part of the map.
- One secret treasure is highlighted on every regular map and contributes to costume unlocks.
- Police Unicorn now has a sixth, larger Boss Bandit who appears only after the five normal bandits are jailed.
- Normal bandits still require 3 hits; Boss Bandit requires 5 hits and retains variable speed.
- Camera now clamps to either the base map or the hidden room, preventing empty black gaps caused by the extended world.

## Preserved from v10/v11
- Two variable-speed patrol people per regular map.
- Rainbow Blast as the only action button.
- Sparkly send-back when the unicorn touches a person.
- Collect-all objective on regular maps.
- In-game menu button.
- Treasure Room and Costume Closet.
